# Product-card brand assets

Product cards reuse the logo files already used by the Brands menu. Matching is case-insensitive and trimmed. HiLook has its own logo rather than inheriting Hikvision's mark. Generic and unknown brands have no invented logo.

Additional assets:

- `public/assets/brands/hilook.svg`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons/main/svg/hilook.svg (source listing: https://dashboardicons.com/icons/hilook).
- `public/assets/brands/ritar.webp`: https://www.ritarpower.com/uploads/image/20251212/ritar-power-logo.webp (Ritar's official website header asset).

Original logo artwork is retained. CSS overlays the logo at the bottom-left without reserving space in the product image area and accommodates blank margins in the supplied Paradox and Arrowhead files. Cards and detail galleries share top-right orange tags with white text. Brand-logo overlays remain exclusive to cards.
